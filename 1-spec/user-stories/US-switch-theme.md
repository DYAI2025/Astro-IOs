# US-switch-theme: Switch Between Dark and Light Mode

**As a** end user, **I want** to toggle between dark and light theme, **so that** I can use the app comfortably in any lighting condition.

**Status**: Approved
**Priority**: Should-have
**Source stakeholder**: [STK-end-user](../stakeholders.md)
**Related goal**: [GOAL-app-store-launch](../goals/GOAL-app-store-launch.md)

## Acceptance Criteria

- Given I am on the Atlas tab, when I tap the theme toggle button, then the UI transitions to the opposite theme
- Given I switch to light mode, then the background becomes warm ivory, text becomes dark brown, cards become white
- Given I restart the app, then my theme preference is preserved
- Given I am in any tab, then the theme applies consistently

## Derived Requirements

- _covered by theme toggle implementation, no additional REQ needed_
