# US-choose-companion: Choose AI Companion

**As a** end user, **I want** to choose between Levi and Eve on a companion selection screen, **so that** I can pick the personality that resonates with me today.

**Status**: Draft
**Priority**: Must-have
**Source stakeholder**: [STK-end-user](../stakeholders.md)
**Related goal**: [GOAL-ai-companions](../goals/GOAL-ai-companions.md)

## Acceptance Criteria

- Given I am on the Companions tab, then I see two cards: Levi (dark blue/gold, ANALYTICAL·CLEAR·PROFOUND) and Eve (violet/silver, DIRECT·IRONIC·HONEST·CLOSE)
- Given I tap a card, then a full-screen conversation view opens with the selected agent
- Given the info hint is visible, then it reads "Levi and Eve share the same knowledge — they only differ in character" in the current language
- Given I tap the back button, then I return to the companion selection
