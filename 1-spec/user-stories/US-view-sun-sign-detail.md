# US-view-sun-sign-detail: View Sun Sign Detail

**As a** end user, **I want** to tap on my sun sign tile and see my sun sign, moon sign, and ascendant explained, **so that** I understand the three pillars of my Western chart without astro jargon.

**Status**: Draft
**Priority**: Must-have
**Source stakeholder**: [STK-end-user](../stakeholders.md)
**Related goal**: [GOAL-fusion-reading](../goals/GOAL-fusion-reading.md)

## Acceptance Criteria

- Given I am on the Atlas tab, when I tap the sun sign tile, then a detail sheet opens
- Given the detail sheet is open, then I see my sun sign hero (icon, name, degree, description), moon sign row with emotional description, and ascendant row with outer-perception description
- Given I swipe down, then the sheet dismisses
