# GOAL-app-store-launch: App Store Launch

**Description**: Ship a polished iOS app to the Apple App Store that passes review, has proper metadata, and provides a complete first-run experience from splash screen to daily use.

**Status**: Draft

**Priority**: Must-have

**Source stakeholder**: [STK-product-owner](../stakeholders.md)

## Success Criteria

- [ ] App icon and launch screen designed and integrated
- [ ] App Store listing: screenshots, description, keywords, privacy policy
- [ ] Apple App Review compliance (no rejected binary)
- [ ] TestFlight beta distribution before public launch
- [ ] Onboarding flow (splash → language → birth data → first reading)
- [ ] No crashes on supported devices (iPhone, iOS 26.2+)
- [ ] Settings screen with sign-out, theme toggle, language switch
